# *Dilophosaurus wetherilli*: research notes

Content: `src/content/species/dilophosaurus-wetherilli.ts` · checked 2026-10-17 · method: [README](README.md)

## Sources and what each supports

| Id | Read | Supports |
|---|---|---|
| nhm-dilophosaurus | Museum page | 7 m, 400 kg (method not stated); Early Jurassic, about 184 Ma; "two-crested lizard"; crests probably keratin-covered, purpose uncertain (recognition or mates), probably too weak for combat; Jesse Williams found the first skeletons, Welles studied them, first thought *Megalosaurus*, renamed after a crested specimen; first crested meat-eating dinosaur, later *Cryolophosaurus*, *Monolophosaurus*, *Guanlong*; more specimens 2001; venom (novel) and frill (film) invented for *Jurassic Park*; desert with rivers and migrating dunes; upper-jaw notch and tooth gap, jaws stronger than first thought, some evidence of preying on *Sarahsaurus*; light build, likely active hunter; feathers unclear (disputed footprints); taxonomy |
| marsh-rowe-2020 | Abstract + open-access full text (Cambridge) | Largest land animal known in Early Jurassic North America; five specimens, one species; crests built from premaxilla ridge, nasal and lacrimal; not a coelophysoid or ceratosaur but a stem-averostran neotheropod; Kayenta Formation silty facies, Sinemurian–Pliensbachian, Navajo Nation, Coconino County; U-Pb 183.7 ± 2.7 Ma for one specimen's level; Jesse Williams found remains in 1940 near Moenave; Welles opened the quarry in 1942; larger individual (UCMP 77270) collected 1964; holotype UCMP 37302; *Sarahsaurus* found directly underneath one skeleton; synonymy (*Megalosaurus wetherilli* 1954 → *Dilophosaurus* 1970) |
| welles-1954 | Crossref record | Original description as *Megalosaurus wetherilli*, *GSA Bulletin* 65: 591 |
| senter-juengst-2016 | Full text (Europe PMC) | UCMP 37302: eight afflicted bones of the pectoral girdle and forelimb, incl. fractured scapula and radius; healing over months, possibly years; right third finger permanently deformed, unable to flex |
| benson-2014 | Supplementary dataset (S011) | Limb-bone circumference masses: UCMP 37302 290 kg, UCMP 77270 349 kg; femur lengths 557 and 610 mm |
| pbdb-dilophosaurus | PBDB API | Kayenta Formation, Coconino County, Arizona; Sinemurian–Pliensbachian stage bounds 199.5–184.2 Ma |

## Measurements

Mass shows all three estimates (290–400 kg): two specimens by limb-bone scaling and the museum figure. Length is the museum figure only; no measured body length was found in the sources read.

## Not used

- Milner et al. 2009 (PLOS ONE): a resting trace from the Moenave Formation of Utah. It is not attributed to *Dilophosaurus*, so it is not cited for this species.
- Welles 1970 (the renaming paper, *J. Paleontology*) has no DOI in Crossref; the renaming is cited through Marsh & Rowe's synonymy list and the NHM page.
- A remembered mass of "283 kg" from the 2020 redescription could not be found in its text; not used.
