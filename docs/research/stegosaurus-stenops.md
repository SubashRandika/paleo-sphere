# *Stegosaurus stenops*: research notes

Content: `src/content/species/stegosaurus-stenops.ts` · checked 2026-10-16 · method: [README](README.md)

## Why *S. stenops*

ICZN Opinion 2320 (2013) replaced the unidentifiable type species *S. armatus* with *S. stenops*. *S. stenops* is also the species of the best specimen ("Sophie"). The NHM Dino Directory still lists "Type species: armatus"; the ICZN Opinion supersedes it.

| nhm-stegosaurus | Museum page | 9 m (genus); 152–145 Ma; plates embedded in skin, scattered in most finds; Marsh's flat-then-upright plates; plate hypotheses; plum-sized brain; tail defence against *Allosaurus* and *Ceratosaurus* (level 3); "Sophie" on display December 2014; taxonomy |

## Measurements

Mass shows **both** estimates for the same specimen, so the quick fact reads 1.08–3.75 t. "How these were estimated" explains that the higher figures are considered too high. Length is the NHM genus figure only.

## Not used

- **Carpenter et al. 2005** (thagomizer wound in an *Allosaurus* vertebra): a book chapter with no DOI and no accessible text, so it could not be verified. This is a strong candidate for a fact once a readable source is found.
- **Lautenschlager et al. 2016** (bite mechanics): its abstract does not state a *Stegosaurus*-specific result in plain terms; Reichel 2010 was used instead.
- **Brain-size "second brain" myth**: no source checked; omitted.
