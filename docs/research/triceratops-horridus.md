# *Triceratops horridus*: research notes

## Sources and what each supports

| Id | Read | Supports |
|---|---|---|
| nhm-triceratops | Museum page | 9 m, 10,000 kg (method not stated); 68–66 Ma; frill nearly 1 m; beak and shearing teeth; low plants and could raise head; healed horn with *Tyrannosaurus* bite marks; frill punctures; "large, pebbled scales"; usually found individually; frill hypotheses; 1887 horn cores as *Bison alticornis*; keratin sheath; "only two species" and skull variety "probably" individual variation; taxonomy |
| pbdb-triceratops-occurrences | PBDB API (166 occurrences) | Hell Creek (MT, SD, ND), Lance (WY, SD), Scollard (AB). Frenchman (SK) records are mostly *T. prorsus*, so left out of the *T. horridus* list |
| pbdb-triceratops-names | PBDB API | *Ceratops horridus* (Marsh 1889) recombined as *T. horridus*; *Bison alticornis* (Marsh 1887) as a nomen dubium in Ceratopsidae; authority reference Marsh 1889, *AJS* 38: 173–175 |
| pbdb-tyrannosaurus | PBDB API | Shared formations with *T. rex* |
| marsh-1889 | Crossref record | Bibliographic data |
| farke-2009 | Abstract (PLOS) | Two brow horns and smaller nasal horn; squamosal (frill) lesions significantly more common than in *Centrosaurus*; "consistent with *Triceratops* using its horns in combat and the frill being adapted as a protective structure" |
| scannella-horner-2010 | Abstract | *Torosaurus* = mature *Triceratops*; 10-year Hell Creek study, > 50 specimens, > 30 skulls |
| longrich-field-2012 | Abstract (PLOS) | *Torosaurus* is a distinct genus |
| scannella-2014 | Abstract (PNAS) | Two species stratigraphically separated; "likely incorporated anagenetic … change" |
| erickson-2015 | Abstract | "a 9-m-long ceratopsian"; teeth wore to form low-friction slicing blades; dental tissues "rivaling the complexity of mammalian dentitions"; "dominant herbivore" |

## Measurements

Length agrees between two sources (9 m). Mass has **only one, museum, figure**, with no method stated; the page labels it that way. A peer-reviewed mass range should be added when found: Campione & Evans 2012 and Sellers et al. 2012 were checked and do not give one for *Triceratops*.

## Disagreements presented

- *Torosaurus* synonymy (2010 vs. 2012).
- Whether *T. horridus* evolved into *T. prorsus* (2014), level 3.

## Not used

- Erickson & Olson 1996 (bite marks): no verifiable abstract.
- D'Anastasio et al. 2022 (combat lesion histology): not needed beyond Farke et al. 2009.
