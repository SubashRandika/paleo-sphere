# *Mamenchisaurus*: research notes

Content: `src/content/species/mamenchisaurus.ts` · checked 2026-10-16 · method: [README](README.md)

## Why a genus-level record

The type species *M. constructus* (Young 1954) is fragmentary, the best-known material belongs to other species (*M. hochuanensis*, *M. sinocanadorum*), and Moore et al. 2023 find the genus **non-monophyletic**. The shortlisted 3D model is not species-specific either. A genus record says honestly what is known; it can be split once the taxonomy settles.

## Disagreements presented

- Taxonomy (Moore et al. 2023).
- Age range: Late Jurassic (NHM) versus Early Cretaceous *M. anyuensis* (Wang et al. 2019). Detrital zircon gives a *maximum* depositional age, hence "suggests".
- Neck posture (Taylor 2022).

## Not used

- Wang et al. 2018 (*GSA Bulletin*, Sichuan basal sauropod ages): abstract not retrievable.
- Mass estimates: none found from a checkable source; the measurement is omitted.
