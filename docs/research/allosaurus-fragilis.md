# *Allosaurus fragilis*: research notes

Content: `src/content/species/allosaurus-fragilis.ts` · checked 2026-10-16 · method: [README](README.md)

## Sources and what each supports

| Id | Read | Supports |
|---|---|---|
| nhm-allosaurus | Museum page | 9.7 m, 2,700 kg (method not stated); 152–145 Ma; dagger-like serrated teeth; prey "*Stegosaurus*, *Diplodocus* and others"; three-fingered hands and horns above the eyes; *Saurophaganax* → *A. anax*; pack-hunting question and *Allosaurus*-on-*Allosaurus* bite marks; horn hypotheses; "flesh grazer"; taxonomy |
| pbdb-allosaurus / pbdb-stegosaurus | PBDB API | Morrison Formation in WY, CO, UT and OK; shared with *Stegosaurus* |
| marsh-1877 | Crossref record | Naming, *AJS* (3) 14: 514–516 |
| rayfield-2001 | Nature abstract | "weak muscle-driven bite force, a very 'light' and 'open' skull architecture and unusually high cranial strength suggests a very specific feeding behaviour" |
| snively-2013 | Abstract | Low (ventral) insertion of *m. longissimus capitis superficialis*, unlike "nearly all other theropods", with over twice the downward (ventroflexive) acceleration; raptor-like head retraction "more probable" than shake-feeding → level 3 |
| hanna-2002 | Abstract | MOR 693, sub-adult, found 1991 in Big Horn County, WY; pathologies on ribs, gastralia, vertebrae, scapula, manus, ilium, metatarsals and pes; behaviours that "likely predisposed" injury |
| bates-2009 | Full text (PDF) | MOR 693 mass "constrained within 1350–1850 kg", best estimate 1,500.91 kg |
| gates-2005 | Abstract | Cleveland-Lloyd: ≥ 70 individuals, ≥ 9 genera, 3:1 predator:prey ratio dominated by *A. fragilis*; drought hypothesis; semi-arid climate with periodic drought |
| peterson-2017 | Abstract | Excavation since the 1920s; competing hypotheses; attritional assemblage in an ephemeral pond during floods |
| chure-loewen-2020 | Full text (Europe PMC) | *A. jimmadseni* from the lower Morrison; nasal crests absent in *A. fragilis*; *Allosaurus* is the most abundant theropod "in virtually all quarries" |
| danison-2024 | Abstract | *Saurophaganax* material is chimeric; theropod part named *A. anax* |

## Measurements

The mass range 1.35–2.7 t combines a sub-adult (Big Al) and the NHM adult figure. The "How these were estimated" list shows each. Length is a single museum figure.

## Not used

- Skin or feather evidence for *Allosaurus*: Hendrickx et al. 2022 (*Biol. Rev.*) likely covers it, but the full text was not reachable. Integument is therefore a level-4 colour note only.
- Carpenter et al. 2005 (*Stegosaurus* spike wound): unverifiable, as noted in the *Stegosaurus* notes.
- A DOI recalled from memory for Rayfield et al. 2001 (`10.1038/35059018`) was wrong; Crossref gives `10.1038/35059070`.
