## Measurements

- **Length:** only Marsh's 1883 estimate was found ("nearly or quite fifty feet", 15–15.2 m). No modern published body length for *B. excelsus* turned up in Tschopp et al. 2015, Taylor & Wedel 2013, Taylor 2022 or the volumetric mass literature, so the article shows Marsh's figure and says when it was made.
- **Mass:** one estimate, 30.5 t, from limb-bone circumferences of the holotype (Benson et al. 2014 dataset).
- **3D model scale:** the model is scaled so its thigh bone roughly matches YPM 1980's 1.725 m femur (read off the model's silhouette, about ±10%), which makes it about 21 m along the backbone. The appearance note says so.

## Not used

- The famous "wrong head" story of early *Brontosaurus*/*Apatosaurus* mounts: not checked against a primary source.
- Marsh's 1883 remark that the skull was smaller than a neck vertebra: his skull identification is uncertain, so it is not repeated.
